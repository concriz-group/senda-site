    Climbing New Zealand

    Activity

    Rock Climbing

    Adventure type

    Overnight trip

    Trip length

    7 days

    Group size

    6

    Difficulty

    Intermediate

    Price

    USD 2,400

    Share this adventure

    E-mail Facebook X LinkedIn

    Overview

    Seven days of guided rock climbing on the South Island, from sunny limestone crags to long alpine ridges with views over turquoise lakes. Every day ends at a lakeside lodge.

    Itinerary

    Two days of skills on single-pitch routes, three days of multi-pitch climbing, and a final alpine summit if the weather allows.

    What to bring

    Climbing shoes, a harness and layers for changing mountain weather. Ropes, helmets and hardware are provided.
