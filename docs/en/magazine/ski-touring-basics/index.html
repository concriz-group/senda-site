    Ski Touring Basics

    Skins on, heels free: ski touring trades the lift queue for a quiet climb and untracked snow. Here is what you need for your first season away from the resort.

    The gear

    Touring bindings, climbing skins and a pair of boots with a walk mode are the core. Add an avalanche transceiver, a shovel and a probe, and learn to use them before you need them.

    Start small

    Pick short, low-angle routes close to the resort, go with someone who has done it before, and check the avalanche bulletin every morning.
